**Mac DFU Studio 2.1 has a new icon, a clearer toolbar, and remembers what it did.**

### New
- **A new icon**: a bolt over a USB-C port, in the same family as IPSW Downloader Plus.
- **History.** Every revive, restore and trip into DFU is kept on your Mac, in the inspector's new History tab, and can be exported as CSV. Nothing leaves the Mac.
- **Notifications and Dock progress.** A notification tells you how a revive or a restore ended while you were doing something else, and the Dock icon shows the percentage. The app asks for permission the first time it opens.
- **Checks before you start.** A welcome sheet on first launch, and Settings › Advanced from then on, list what the app needs: notifications, an administrator account, its signed helper, a DFU port, Apple Configurator, an internet connection. Starting a revive or a restore on battery or offline now asks first.
- **About and support.** Settings › About shows the version, how this copy is signed, and the Mac it runs on, with links to the website, the source, and the donation pages.
- **Save Diagnostic Report…** writes the report to a file, next to Copy.
- **Firmware from IPSW Downloader Plus 27.2.** The restore sheet shows compatible images already in your library. You can also open the downloader on the connected Mac’s model or open an IPSW from Finder. Confirm in Mac DFU Studio when you are ready to restore.
- The Retina installer has a violet USB-C illustration and drag-to-install instructions in English and Italian.

### Changed
- **Toolbar.** The port menu now says where the port is in words, with a bolt on the DFU port ("Left, front" instead of "Left Front · DFU"). The "…" button is a labelled **Tools** menu, and it sits at the trailing edge with the inspector button.
- **Macs.** The orange mark on some models now says what it means: the DFU port is not where the other models of that family have it.
